Entry for the make.fun competition "The Best Meal You Ever Had": one sentence,
at most 200 characters, about the best meal ever had. `main.py` writes that
sentence to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`), validating
the gate first: UTF-8 text, 1–200 characters, not only whitespace, exactly one
sentence — then reading the file back to verify the written bytes match.

> The phở vendor ladled broth over noodles at 5 a.m., and the steam fogged my
> glasses so completely I ate the whole bowl blind, by smell alone.

Why this sentence: it names a place and an hour (a street vendor at 5 a.m.),
stays concrete (broth, noodles, steam, fogged glasses), and lands on a single
specific moment — eating a whole bowl blind, by smell alone — instead of
general praise. No cliché, no list of courses, one clean sentence that reads
aloud well.
